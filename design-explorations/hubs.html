Hubs

Independent Meeple Cart hubs for special ways to trade and share games. They sit beside the board, not in it — run by members, free like everything else.

HUB · OPEN

Gift Cascades

PAY IT FORWARD

A cascade starts when one member gifts a game to another — no payment, no trade-back. The receiver keeps the chain alive by gifting a game of their own onward to the next person, and the hub records every link.

Chains have no fixed length; a cascade runs for as long as its members keep gifting.

Trade Parties

ONE ROUND

An organised community event: many members each bring a list of games they're offering and games they're after, and every list goes into a single round of matching. The hub works out a set of trades across the whole group, so each game moves once and everyone trades at the same time.

  1. Bring a list

    Before the party closes, you post the games you'd part with and the games you'd want in return.

  2. Lists are matched

    All lists are matched together in one round — including longer loops your own two-way swaps would never find.

  3. Everyone trades at once

    Matches are announced, and each member posts or hands over one game and receives one back.

Group Buys

SPLIT THE FREIGHT

When a game only ships from overseas, members pool their orders into one carton and split the freight between them. One member hosts the buy and places the order; everyone pays their own share up front.

When the carton lands, the host posts each copy onward within New Zealand.
