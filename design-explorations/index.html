Meeple Cart — five redesign directions

Same surface every time (the browse / offers feed), five committed visual worlds. All are faithful to the product: free, community-run, NZ-wide, trust in the core. Open each full-screen; try narrow widths too.

Merged prototype — Club Night, House Colours

  1. →Board

    The pinned-offers feed — the home surface. Momo/Inter fonts, Board · Browse · Chat · Hubs nav, rectangular angled trust stamps. Card titles link to the offer page.

  2. →Browse

    The full catalogue: search + deep filters over everything currently available, in compact density.

  3. →Chat

    Community discussions — categories and threads in the same world.

  4. →Hubs

    Gift Cascades, Trade Parties, Group Buys — the secondary programmes' home.

  5. →Offer detail

    One offer up close: gallery, bundle items, terms, lister trust panel, message CTA.

  6. →Your profile

    The signed-in member's own view: identity and trust stamp, their pins with management actions, trades in motion, vouches received, watchlist. Reached from the header avatar.

  7. →Pin a listing

    The creation flow: listing → offer → items, photos, terms, with a live preview of the board card in a sticky rail. Reached from the header button.
